Reincarnation of an original VHS SkillTapes mod for b42 stable.
Adds VHS tapes for non-combat skills. Each series has 6 episodes that give XP.
All tapes spawn in VHS retail stores among other reels.

## Features

> ! The mod does not repair original tapes; treat Reels! as a brand-new mod for your save.

Adds VHS tapes for such skills:
- Carpentry
- Cooking
- Electrical
- Mechanics
- Tailoring
- Welding
- Agriculture
- First Aid
- Fishing
- Foraging
- Trapping

## Rationale

For some reason, the vanilla game lacks VHSes for many skills. In addition, the original mod was removed by the mod author, even though it had tons of subscribers (including me). So, I decided to reimplement the original mod in my own way.
