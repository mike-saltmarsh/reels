Reincarnation of an original **VHS SkillTapes** mod, but for **b42 stable**.  
Adds VHS tapes for non-combat skills. Each series has 6 episodes that give XP.  
All tapes spawn in VHS retail stores among other reels.

## Features

>⚠️ The mod does not repair original tapes from **VHS SkillTapes**; treat Reels! as a brand-new mod for your game save.

Adds VHS tapes for the following skills:

* Carpentry  
* Cooking  
* Electrical  
* Mechanics  
* Tailoring  
* Welding  
* Agriculture  
* First Aid  
* Fishing  
* Foraging  
* Trapping
* Carving
* Knapping
* Masonry
* Pottery

## Usage

It is _save-safe_, but you won't find any reels in already visited areas unless you have loot respawn turned on.  
You can also safely disable this mod mid-gameplay. The reels will simply become unusable. Once you disable the mod and save the game, all your reels will become pumpkins and mice forever.  
It should also be fully _MP compatible_, but it needs additional testing.

## Rationale

For some reason, the vanilla game lacks VHSes for many skills. In addition, the mod author removed the original mod, even though it had tons of subscribers (including me). So, I decided to reimplement the original mod in my own way.
